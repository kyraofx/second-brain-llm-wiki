# Catalog

### Areas

- `technology` — software, AI, and digital systems
- `business` — organizations, products, strategy, and operations
- `arts` — design, media, and creative practice
- `professional-life` — careers, work, and professional development
- `relationships` — personal and professional connections
- `personal-life` — routines, recreation, and everyday responsibilities

### Sensitivity

- `public` — synthetic material explicitly safe for this demonstration repository

### Record types

- `project` — finite outcome with status and lifecycle dates
- `person` — a durable personal or professional identity
- `event` — a significant occasion with independent identity
- `concept` — evergreen explanatory knowledge
- `conversation` — an exchange worth revisiting
- `how-to` — a reusable procedure
- `organization` — a company, nonprofit, community, or similar entity
- `place` — a physical location with durable identity
- `podcast` — a show-level profile with dated episode entries
- `tool` — a software product, platform, or operating utility
- `video` — a creator- or channel-level profile with dated entries
